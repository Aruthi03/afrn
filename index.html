Dear Afrin...

First standard-la new-a vandha oru ponnu...

Enkuda appo yaarum pesa maataanga. Nee dhaan vandhu pesuna. Naan lusu maari “enga appa pesakoodathu nu sonnaru” nu solliruken. 😭 Nee usually neraiya things-ah marandhuruva. Aana idhu mattum innum nyabagam vechuruka.

Adhu dhaan enaku sometimes romba special-ah irukum. Namma friendship start aana first moment-ah nee remember pannura.

Adhukku apram nee en before roll number. Injection podumbodhu nee romba bayapaduva. Roll-number wise dhaan irupom. Nee azhuga start panniruppa; naan unakku console panna try panniruppen.

Approm benchmates. Anga dhaan namma fight department officially start. 😂 Most of our fights-ku meaning-ey irukaadhu. Un mood poruthu mood swings aagi sandai varum. Nee pesama irundha naan romba bayapaduven, silent-ah iruppen, romba feel pannuven. Possessive na enna nu theriyadha vayasula kooda naan un mela avlo possessive-a irundhirukken pola.

6th standard-la extracurricular class-ku pogama one full hour mam kitta pesitu irundhom. Next hour place change panna sollitaanga. Appo mam Dharani-ya naan ninaichu un pakathula ukkara vechutanga. Apo nee kudutha reaction innum enaku nyabagam iruku. Enakum kastama irundhuchu. Again mam-ku strike aagi place change pannitaanga.

Namma Sakar movie-ku poitu vandha Sunday. Naan noon show ponen, nee evening show pona. Next day vandhu adha pathi pesinom. Approm Kannukkul Nikkuriye, Kadhaliye and pala album songs ellam paaduvom. Unoda RA RAHHH reaction. 😂

Lunch-la ukkaradhukku sandai. Approm nammaloda uppu kari side dish. Adhu namma vecha name. Ippo vera yaaravadhu andha name use panna enaku apdiye tension aagum. Vechadhu namma dhaane! 😭

Next 7th — Mageshwari ma'am class. Neraiya nadandhuchu. Good things to remember and some to forget. 😂 Kiss Day vandhuchu. Nee enakku multiple times kiss pannitu, enaiyum panna sollikitta. Aana naan kadaisi varaikum pannave illa nu ninaikuren. SORRYYYY. Heeheehee. 🤭 Approm baby powder smell vechu kandupudhicha. HOW?! 😭 Approm Slap Day. Ellarum adichutu, mam ta thittu vangitu... and so on.

Later March 16. Eve school leave nu message. Namma conference call potu besties group create pannom. Adhula neraiya problems vandhudhu. Yeppa yeppa. 😂

And yeah... lockdown. Naan neraiya lusu maari pannirupen. Adha mention panna virumbala. Nee-yum marandhuru. 😭

After years, 21 September 2021 — we re-entered our school as 9th standard. Again you became my benchmate. But appo naan ella pesave illa. Adhuku romba romba sorry. Ippo na feel panren.

In January nee “I'm going to leave the school” nu sonna. Adha sonna udaney avlo dhaan. Enaku aariyamaiye alugai vandhuduchu. Adhe time nu paatha place-um change pannitaanga.

Approm our last meet February 1, 2022 nu maariduchu. En birthday-ku nee video eduthu ellaam send panna.

Andha time-la start aana long distance ipo varaikum thodarudhu. Innum thodarum nu namburen.

November 14, 2022 — 10th standard-la namma meet panom. Maybe 5 mins irukumah. Aprm avlo dhaan.

June 7, 2023 — again after a year, maybe 5 mins pathu pesirupom.

After that July 28, 2026 la dhaan again pathom.

Ippo varaikum “oru nalla photo” nu solra alavuku namma kitta enna iruku? 😭 Proper photo illa. But memories? Romba iruku.

Sometimes nee ellame marandhuruva nu enaku thonum. So I wanted to make something that you can come back to and remember.

Hey. Look. We actually have a whole story. 🥹

1st standard-la en kitta vandhu pesina andha ponnu... still en best friend-ah iruka. ❤️
